## Commmemorating 85 Years: A Legacy of Resilience

Join us as we mark the 85th Anniversary of [Executive Order 9066](https://encyclopedia.densho.org/Executive_Order_9066/) and honor the
Japanese and Japanese Americans who were forcibly removed from their homes
and unjustly incarcerated during World War II, including the over 7,500 held at
the Puyallup Assembly Center on the site of what was then the Puyallup
Fairgrounds.

**Their stories must never be forgotten.**
 
**Their history must never be erased.**

## Event Details
* **Date:** Saturday, January 30, 2027
* **Location:** The **Expo Hall** at the [Washington State Fair](https://thefair.com)
* **Time:** 10am – 12pm, Doors open at 9:30am
* **Park:** Gold Parking Lot
